What we do

Electrical work for homes, shops and workshops, plus electronics repair and engineering advice.

  • House and building wiring

    New wiring, rewiring of old homes, concealed and surface conduit, distribution boards and load planning so circuits are not overloaded.

  • Electrical fault finding

    Tripping breakers, short circuits, flickering lights, voltage drops, hot sockets and earth leakage. We trace the cause instead of replacing parts at random.

  • Earthing and protection

    Earthing, MCBs and RCCBs, surge protection and safe grounding of appliances, so a fault trips a breaker instead of shocking someone.

  • Power backup

    UPS and inverter connections and generator changeover switches, installed so backup power never feeds back into the line.

  • Electronics repair and supply

    Power supplies, control boards, UPS and inverter faults, sensors and controllers. We also source components and advise on the right ratings.

  • Engineering and inspection

    Load calculations, single-line diagrams, material lists and cost estimates, and a written check of existing wiring before you buy or rent a property.

Also available on request: lighting and fan installation.

Problems we fix in Pakistani homes and shops

Voltage ups and downs

Fridges, ACs and TVs burn out when the supply swings. We check the line and fit protection and stabilisers rated for the load.

Load shedding backup

UPS, inverter or generator wired so the fans, lights and fridge stay on, and power never feeds back into the line.

Shocks and missing earthing

A tingle from the fridge, geyser or washing machine body means earthing is missing or faulty. We test it and fix it.

Old and overloaded wiring

Undersized cable, extension-board chains and several ACs on one circuit. We rewire to the real load.

How a job runs

The same five steps every time, so you know what happens and what it costs before we touch anything.

  1. You message us

    Describe the problem and send photos of the wiring, board or device.

  2. We check the site

    We look at the fault in person or review your photos, and measure what needs measuring.

  3. You get an estimate

    Work and materials are listed and agreed before we start.

  4. We do the work

    Power is isolated, work follows the agreed plan, and the area is left clean.

  5. We test and hand over

    Each circuit is tested, and we show you what was done and how to use it safely.

Electrical emergency?

Switch off the main breaker, keep away from wet areas and exposed wires, and do not touch anything that is sparking or smoking. If anyone is hurt, call emergency services first, then message us.

Questions people ask

What should I send on WhatsApp?

A short description of the problem, your area, and one or two clear photos or a short video of the wiring, board or device. The more we can see, the better the first answer.

Will I know the cost before work starts?

Yes. We list the work and materials and agree the price with you first. If we find something extra once the work is open, we tell you before going ahead.

Do you supply the materials?

We can supply cable, breakers, switches and fittings, or you can buy them yourself. Either way we tell you the correct size and rating so the wiring matches the load.

Can you fix a device, not just wiring?

We repair power supplies, UPS and inverter faults and control boards. If a repair is not worth the cost, we say so rather than charging you for it. Prices are quoted in PKR.

Why do my breakers keep tripping?

Usually an overloaded circuit, a faulty appliance, damaged insulation or moisture. Do not replace the breaker with a bigger one. Message us and we will find the actual cause.
